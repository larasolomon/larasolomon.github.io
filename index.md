## About

Lara Solomon is an Australian healthcare business owner and operations manager based in Sydney, New
South Wales. She owns and manages [Rose Bay Medical Clinic](https://www.rosebaymedicalclinic.com.au/),
a general practice in Rose Bay in Sydney's eastern suburbs, and runs operations and customer
management for [Redrew Clothing](https://redrewclothing.com/), a sustainable womenswear label designed
and made in Sydney.

Outside of business, she works in enumerative combinatorics. In July 2026 the
[On-Line Encyclopedia of Integer Sequences](https://oeis.org/) credited her with the thirteenth terms
of two long-standing sequences counting self-avoiding paths on square grid graphs — results that had
stood unextended for years, on a problem the encyclopedia classifies as computationally hard.

Two very different disciplines, one habit: taking a system that nobody has fully mapped and working
out exactly how it behaves.

## Published Mathematics — On-Line Encyclopedia of Integer Sequences

**Contributor — three sequences**

The OEIS is the reference database of integer sequences, founded in 1964 by Neil Sloane and cited
across mathematics and computer science. Lara's contributions all concern the same stubborn problem:
counting **self-avoiding paths** on a square grid graph — every route through an *n* × *n* lattice
that never revisits a point. The count explodes so violently with *n* that each additional term is
its own computational project.

### [A236753](https://oeis.org/A236753) — a(13), 30 July 2026

*Number of simple (non-intersecting) directed paths on the grid graph P_n × P_n.*

Flagged `hard` by the OEIS. Lara computed the 13 × 13 case — a 39-digit number:

```
779903870849450440101587633989096314113
```

### [A288032](https://oeis.org/A288032) — a(13), 30 July 2026

*Number of (undirected) paths in the n × n grid graph.*

The undirected companion result, contributed the same day. The two values are self-checking: the
directed count is exactly twice the undirected count plus the 169 single-vertex paths of a 13 × 13
grid.

```
389951935424725220050793816994548156972
```

### [A288518](https://oeis.org/A288518) — b-file extended to 351 terms

*Array read by antidiagonals: T(m,n) = number of (undirected) paths in the grid graph P_m × P_n.*

The general rectangular case. Lara extended the sequence's data file from 276 terms to **351** —
adding three complete antidiagonals of new results to a table that had stood at 23 since 2017.
