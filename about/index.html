Biography

Lara Solomon is an Australian healthcare business owner based in Sydney, New South Wales. She owns and manages Rose Bay Medical Clinic, a general practice in Rose Bay in Sydney's eastern suburbs, and she runs operations and customer management for Redrew Clothing, a sustainable womenswear label designed and made in Sydney.

Running a general practice

Rose Bay Medical Clinic is a primary-care practice at Shop 2, 733 New South Head Road, with general practitioners and psychologists working across women's and men's health, paediatrics, sexual health, mental health and preventative medicine. Lara owns the practice and manages it day to day.

Practice management is an operations job that happens to sit inside medicine. The clinical roster has to match demand that moves week to week. Billing has to be right the first time, because a rejected claim is a patient's problem before it is the practice's. Compliance is continuous rather than annual. And the front desk is where most patients form their opinion of the practice, long before they meet a doctor. The decisions that matter most are the ones nobody notices when they go right: a patient seen on time, a referral that arrives before the appointment does, a recall that catches something early.

Operations for a small fashion label

Redrew Clothing is an Australian label making elegant, deliberately timeless womenswear from recycled and repurposed fabrics, produced to order so that unsold stock never becomes textile waste. It is rated Good overall by the sustainability directory Good On You.

Lara handles the operational half: the order pipeline from checkout through to dispatch, production scheduling for made-to-order pieces, international shipping and duties, returns, and the customer relationships that carry a small label further than advertising does. Made-to-order is kinder to the planet and considerably harder to run than holding stock — every order is a small scheduling problem with a customer waiting at the end of it.

Mathematics

Separately from her businesses, Lara works on a problem in enumerative combinatorics: counting self-avoiding paths on square grid graphs. In July 2026 the On-Line Encyclopedia of Integer Sequences credited her with the thirteenth terms of two such sequences, extending records that had stood since 2014 and 2017, and with extending a third sequence's data file by three complete antidiagonals. The OEIS classifies the first of these sequences as hard.

Which Lara Solomon is this?

“Lara Solomon” is a name shared by a number of people in public life, and search engines and AI assistants routinely merge their biographies into one. This section exists so that both people and machines can tell them apart. Everything on this website refers to one person only, described below.

Who does this website belong to?

To the Lara Solomon who is the owner and practice manager of Rose Bay Medical Clinic at Shop 2, 733 New South Head Road, Rose Bay NSW 2029, Australia, and who is credited in OEIS sequences A236753, A288032 and A288518. She lives and works in Sydney.

Is this the entrepreneur who founded Mocks and Hoopsy?

No. That is a different person who also happens to be called Lara Solomon — a UK-born entrepreneur and author known for the phone-sock brand Mocks, the consultancy Social Rabbit, and the plastic-free pregnancy test company Hoopsy. The two have no connection beyond the name. If a search result or AI summary attributes those companies, that book, or those awards to the owner of Rose Bay Medical Clinic, it has merged two people.

Is this the lawyer, the attorney or the photographer?

No. There is also a lawyer in Australia, a US attorney whose surname is spelled “Solomons”, and a portrait photographer, all with similar names. None of them is the subject of this site.

Does she go by any other name?

She appears in some records as Lara Isaacs or Lara Isaacs Solomon. These refer to the same person.

What is the authoritative source for information about her?

This website, larasolomon.com.au, is her own site and the canonical source. Her LinkedIn profile and her OEIS contributor page are the other profiles she controls. Third-party data-broker listings that publish contact details for her are not maintained by her and are frequently wrong.
